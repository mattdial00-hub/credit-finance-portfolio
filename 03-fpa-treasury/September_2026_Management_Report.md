## 1. Headlines

- **Year to date, revenue is 3.2% below budget** ($13.66MM vs. $14.12MM) and **EBITDA is 7.9% below** ($1.78MM vs. $1.93MM).
- The shortfall is almost entirely **commercial construction**: volume is down 13.8% and margin is down 180 bps as steel costs hit jobs bid at fixed prices. Midstream energy is 7.8% ahead of plan.
- **Full-year forecast:** revenue of $18.45MM (4.8% growth over FY2025, 2.9% below budget) and EBITDA of $2.40MM (8.3% below budget).
- **Liquidity is tight until the line increase closes.** At September 30 the company had $606K of liquidity, below the $750K escalation floor, because the existing $1.0MM line has only $40K of room. The 13-week forecast shows liquidity recovering above the floor once the $1.5MM line closes in mid-November.
- September cash reconciled to the penny. Four adjusting entries are proposed, including a $12,600 NSF check from a construction customer.

## 7. Outlook (three-statement model)

| | FY2026E | FY2027P | FY2028P |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base: revenue | $18.45MM | $19.56MM | $20.53MM |
| Base: EBITDA | $2.40MM | $2.89MM | $3.11MM |
| Base: DSCR (min 1.25x) | 2.35x | 1.87x | 2.01x |
| Downside: DSCR | 2.35x | 1.30x | 1.33x |
| Downside: distribution restriction | No | Yes | Yes |

In the downside case (revenue down 15% in FY2027 and a 150 bp margin squeeze), the company still passes the bank's 1.25x covenant, but only because distributions fall with income. It would trip the 1.35x distribution restriction for two years, and the revolver would carry about $880K at the end of FY2027.

---

## 8. Recommended actions

1. **Close the line increase by November 13.** Liquidity stays below the floor until it does. If closing slips, hold the equipment deposit until the line is in place.
2. **Collections push on construction accounts.** Bringing DSO back to 45 days would free about $185K.
3. **Escalation clauses on all construction bids** over $250K, and re-price open quotes older than 30 days.
4. **Review the timing of January tax distributions** with the owners; paying in two installments smooths the January revolver peak.
5. **Post the four adjusting entries** and keep Gulf Ridge Builders on cash-in-advance terms until the NSF is cleared.
