## 4. Watch list

Relationships scoring 3 or more on the early warning scorecard, ranked by score.

### 1. Heights Craft Brewing Co. | $2.05MM | Grade 6 to 8 | Score 13, Critical

**What happened:** Lost its largest distribution contract in Q2; revenue down 22%. Payment is 32 days past due. DSCR is 0.94x against a 1.20x minimum, and liquidity is $60K against a $150K minimum. Annual statements arrived 41 days late, and the last borrowing base certificate is 72 days old. Collateral coverage is 0.92x.

**Action plan:**
- Transfer to Special Assets
- Order equipment appraisal
- Obtain updated guarantor personal financial statement
- Evaluate forbearance agreement with defined milestones

### 2. Westchase Office Plaza, LLC | $11.20MM | Grade 5 to 7 | Score 7, Critical

**What happened:** Occupancy fell from 86% to 71% after the anchor tenant vacated. DSCR dropped to 1.12x against 1.25x. A new appraisal puts LTV at 79% against a 75% maximum, and collateral coverage is below 1.00x. Loan matures December 2027.

**Action plan:**
- Require a $600K principal curtailment or additional collateral to restore 75% LTV
- Monthly leasing activity reports
- Begin refinance planning now, 15 months ahead of maturity

### 3. Harbor Point Logistics, LLC | $5.20MM | Grade 4 to 5 | Score 6, Critical

**What happened:** Freight rate softness cut EBITDA 14%. DSCR fell to 1.18x against 1.20x, and leverage is 3.30x, close to the 3.50x maximum. Annual statements were 22 days late and June 30 interims are 47 days past due.

**Action plan:**
- Default notice issued for covenant breach and late reporting
- Interim statements required within 15 days
- Evaluate sale of excess tractors to reduce debt

### 4. Lone Oak Hospitality, Inc. | $7.95MM | Grade 5 to 6 | Score 5, Critical

**What happened:** A new competing hotel opened nearby and RevPAR fell 9%. DSCR is 1.08x against 1.25x. The guarantor liquidity covenant still passes at $610K.

**Action plan:**
- Covenant waiver with fee
- Six-month debt service reserve funded by the borrower
- 90-day business plan and monthly STR market reports

### 5. Cypress Ridge Apartments, LP | $13.70MM | Grade 4 to 5 | Score 3, Watch

**What happened:** Insurance premiums rose 38% and concessions on new leases compressed NOI. DSCR fell from 1.38x to 1.21x, within 1% of the 1.20x minimum. This is the largest relationship in the portfolio.

**Action plan:**
- Monthly rent rolls
- Sponsor-funded insurance reserve
- Renew the interest rate cap before its March 2027 expiration
