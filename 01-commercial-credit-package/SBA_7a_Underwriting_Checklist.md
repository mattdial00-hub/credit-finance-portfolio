# SBA 7(a) Underwriting Checklist

A working checklist for screening and underwriting an SBA 7(a) request. Use it alongside the bank's credit policy.

> **Rules change.** SBA published SOP 50 10 8.1 on August 14, 2026, effective October 1, 2026. It replaces SOP 50 10 8 for loans that receive an SBA loan number on or after that date. Always confirm each item against the current SOP and any procedural notices at [sba.gov](https://www.sba.gov/document/sop-50-10-lender-development-company-loan-programs).

**Applied to Brazos Bend:** the request was underwritten as a conventional loan. The borrower has adequate collateral, equity, and cash flow, so it would likely not meet SBA's "credit elsewhere" test. This checklist is included to show SBA program knowledge for lenders that run both channels.

---

## 1. Eligibility screening

- [ ] Operating, for-profit business located in the U.S.
- [ ] Meets SBA size standard for its NAICS code (employees or revenue)
- [ ] Not an ineligible business type (passive real estate, lending, speculative, etc.)
- [ ] **Credit elsewhere test:** documented reason the borrower cannot get the same credit on reasonable terms without the guaranty
- [ ] **Ownership and citizenship:** every direct and indirect owner and required guarantor checked against the SOP's current definition of an Ineligible Person (Appendix 3). This area was revised by procedural notice in December 2025 and again in SOP 50 10 8.1
- [ ] Use of proceeds is eligible (working capital, equipment, real estate, acquisition, eligible refinance)
- [ ] No delinquent federal debt; CAIVRS check clear
- [ ] Character review of owners per SOP requirements

## 2. Loan size and program

- [ ] Loan amount within program maximum
- [ ] **7(a) Small Loan ($350,000 or less):** SBSS score at or above the SBA minimum (raised to 165 under SOP 50 10 8)
- [ ] Standard 7(a) (over $350,000): full lender underwriting and credit memo
- [ ] Maturity matches useful life of assets financed

## 3. Equity injection

- [ ] Startups and changes of ownership: minimum 10% equity injection
- [ ] Source of injection verified (bank statements, gift letters, seller note terms if allowed)
- [ ] Post-closing liquidity documented for buyers in acquisitions

## 4. Repayment ability

- [ ] Business DSCR meets SBA and bank minimums on historical or reasonable projected cash flow
- [ ] Global cash flow including all owners required to guarantee
- [ ] Projections supported by stated assumptions for startups and acquisitions
- [ ] Owner compensation and living expenses reasonable and accounted for

## 5. Collateral

- [ ] Fully secured to the extent possible with available business assets
- [ ] Personal real estate taken when business collateral is insufficient, per SOP thresholds
- [ ] Lien positions confirmed by UCC and title search
- [ ] Not declined solely for lack of collateral

## 6. Guaranties

- [ ] Unlimited personal guaranty from each owner of 20% or more
- [ ] Spouse guaranty or limited guaranty where required by SOP

## 7. Verification and documentation

- [ ] IRS tax transcript verification (Form 4506-C) for business returns
- [ ] Personal financial statements for all guarantors (SBA Form 413 or equivalent)
- [ ] Borrower information form (SBA Form 1919)
- [ ] Business financial statements: three years plus current interim
- [ ] Debt schedule tied to the balance sheet
- [ ] A/R and A/P agings if working capital is involved

## 8. Closing requirements

- [ ] Hazard insurance on collateral
- [ ] Life insurance on key owners where required by SOP
- [ ] Settlement sheet (SBA Form 1050) at disbursement
- [ ] Guaranty fee calculated and collected
- [ ] Authorization conditions satisfied before disbursement
